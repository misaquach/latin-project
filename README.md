This is a website intending to help people with Latin declension through a simple game. Basically, a prompt appears and you type the declination of the given noun according to its grammatical case.
This was created with C.S.S., H.T.M.L., and JavaScript.
How to use:
As of now, just choose one of the links under the 'Declination practice' section, and as a prompt appears, type in the proper form of the Latin noun. Click 'hint' for guide to verb endings.
